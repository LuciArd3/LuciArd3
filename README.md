# 💫 About Me:
🔭 I am developing a comprehensive software platform that evaluates listed companies to automatically determine if their stock is undervalued or overvalued. Designed for retail investors, the system bridges the gap between complex quantitative analysis and accessible investing by explaining what makes a business stronger or weaker over time.
